# TrioWay App Store Listing Draft

## Short Description

Three-colour geometric watchface for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2, with five layouts, optional dates and thirteen colour presets.

## Full Description

TrioWay is a watchface for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2 that blends clean geometry with practical time layouts.

Features:

- Five layouts: Analogue, Analogue Numerals, Analogue Marks, Hybrid, and Digital Large
- Three dynamic background sectors driven by hour and minute position
- Hybrid places the exact hour and minute at the outer edge for quick reading
- Numbered analogue highlights the current hour and nearest minute numeral on a 1–12 dial
- Optional date display with placement adjusted for the selected layout
- Optional analogue hands
- Thirteen colour presets, from restrained neutrals to Dusk, Lagoon and Ember
- Optional subtle texture, off by default
- Full colour customisation for sectors, hands, and complications
